# Content Guide

## Two-dimensional classification
Every public article has exactly one topic and one kind.

### Topic: what the article is about
- `programming`: programming, CS courses, tools, engineering practice
- `ai-systems`: LLMs, agents, workflows, SJTUClaw, WorkBuddy
- `finance`: finance courses, investment, asset pricing, financial engineering
- `mathematics`: mathematics, probability, statistics, econometrics
- `trading`: trading review, market judgment, mistakes, risk rules
- `career`: internships, recruiting, work methods, stage summaries

### Kind: how the article is written
- `note`: structured learning note
- `tutorial`: reproducible procedure or explanation
- `project-log`: project progress and technical decisions
- `review`: post-mortem or retrospective
- `essay`: complete argument or long-form reflection
- `field-note`: observation from internships, interviews, or practical work

## Publication standard
A public article should be accurate, readable, and reusable. Raw notes remain drafts.
